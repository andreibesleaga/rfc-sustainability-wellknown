# SFC profile, version 2.0

SFC 2 (Sustainability-First Consensus profile, version 2.0) is the one profile text for ledger records, their sums, the declaration at `/.well-known/sustainability-data` and the checks a reader can run.

The text: <https://github.com/andreibesleaga/awesome-blockchain-greentech/blob/main/Innovative%20Projects/SFC_COMPLIANCE.md>

This folder holds the checker (`sfc-check.mjs`, `lib/`), the schemas, the test vectors and the examples. See `README.md`.

Earlier profile texts stay at the commits cited in the spec's history section.
